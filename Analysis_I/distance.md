# Distance
**Definition:** Let $x$ and $y$ be two [[rational_number|rational]] (or [[real_number|real]]) numbers. The quantity $|x - y|$ is called the *distance* between $x$ and $y$, sometimes denoted $d(x, y)$.

Basic properties of distance and absolute value:
1. **Non-degeneracy of absolute value:** We have $|x| \geq 0$ and $|x| = 0$ iff $x = 0$.
2. **Triangle inequality for absolute value:** $|x + y| \leq |x| + |y|$
3. We have $-y \leq x \leq y$ iff $|x| \leq y$. In particular, we have $|-x| \leq x \leq |x|$.
4. **Multiplicativity of absolute value:** $|xy| = |x| |y|$. In particular, $|x| = |-x|$.
5. **Non-degeneracy of distance:** We have $d(x, y) \geq 0$ and $d(x, y) = 0$ iff $x = y$.
6. **Symmetry of distance:** We have $d(x, y) = d(y, x)$.
7. **Triangle inequality for distance:** We have $d(x, z) \leq d(x, y) + d(y, z)$.
