# Bounded Sequences
**Defintion**: Let $M \geq 0$ be rational. A finite [[sequence]] $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ is *bounded by* $M$ iff $a_i \leq M$ for all $1 \leq i \leq n$. An infinite sequence $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ is *bounded by* $M$ iff $a_i \leq M$ for all $i \geq 1$.

Every finite sequence is bounded.

Every [[cauchy_sequence|Cauchy sequence]] is bounded.
